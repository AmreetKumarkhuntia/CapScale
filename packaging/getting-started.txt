Getting started with CapScale

CapScale shows your console through a capture card, upscaled to your monitor and
optionally smoothed to a higher frame rate. Everything runs on your graphics card.

Getting a picture
  1. Connect the console's HDMI to the capture card, and the card to this PC by USB.
  2. Close OBS, Discord streaming, or anything else using the card.
  3. Start CapScale. On the Source tab pick the card, 1920 x 1080 and 60,
     then press Start capture.
  4. The picture opens in its own window. F11 toggles fullscreen, Escape stops.

Making it look better (tabs in the settings window)
  Picture     the upscaler, sharpening, colour grading and a .cube LUT.
  Frame rate  in-between frames, for example a 30 fps game shown at 60.
  Output      window size, vsync and the stats overlay.
  Audio       the game's sound; raise the delay if sound comes before the picture.

Keys (on the picture window)
  F1 settings   F2 stats   F3 before/after split   F4 next upscaler
  F11 fullscreen   Escape stop

Changes apply at once. Press "Save to config.toml" to keep them for next time.
Settings live in %APPDATA%\CapScale.

AI frame generation (optional, NVIDIA RTX only)
  If you ticked it during setup, it is ready: tick "AI frames (RIFE)" on the
  Frame rate tab. To add it later, run "CapScale AI setup" from the Start menu
  (about 4 GB download), then restart CapScale.

The same guide is on the "Getting started" tab inside the app.
